The CPCS card route

Red card
to Blue card.

The Trained Operator (Red) card lasts two years and cannot be renewed. The Competent Operator (Blue) card lasts five years and can. This is how you get from one to the other.

  1. Pass the CITB test

    Pass the relevant CITB Health, Safety and Environment Test. You can sit it with us in Bargeddie.

  2. CPCS Theory Test

    Pass the CPCS Theory Test within two years of your CITB test.

  3. CPCS Practical Test

    Pass the CPCS Practical Test within six months of the Theory Test, and within two years of the CITB test.

  4. Red Trained Operator card

    Your first card. It is valid for two years and cannot be renewed, so the next step matters.
    2 years · non-renewable

  5. Level 2 Plant Operations NVQ

    Completing the relevant NVQ or SVQ units is the key step towards the Blue card. We assess Level 2 Plant Operations NVQs for all major items of plant machinery, with monthly payment plans available.

  6. Blue Competent Operator card

    Typically valid for five years. You do not normally need another NVQ to renew it. A Blue card can also be obtained directly.
    5 years · renewable
