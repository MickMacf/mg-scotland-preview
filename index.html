Courses

Six routes
onto site.

Quality training at competitive prices, for the construction, civil engineering, facilities management, utilities, local authority and oil and gas sectors.

Plant operator card

CPCS

The preferred operator skills card for the plant sector. Start with the Red Trained Operator card and progress to the Blue Competent Operator card.

CPCS training Plant operator card

NPORS

Accredited plant training for all industries, plus driving skills (4x4, ATVs), groundcare, and forestry and arboriculture. Cards are valid for 3 or 5 years.

NPORS training SCQF level 4

SQA

The 1-day SQA Award in Health and Safety in a Construction Environment, needed for the CSCS Green Labourers Card. Also SVQs and SQA-approved assessor training.

SQA courses Level 2 and Level 3

NVQ

Level 2 Plant Operations NVQs assessed for all major items of plant machinery, Groundworks NVQ Level 2 and Supervisor NVQ Level 3. Flexible payment plans are available.

NVQ details Half day or full day

In-House

Short awareness courses such as abrasive wheels, asbestos awareness, manual handling and harness training, held at our premises or on your site.

All short courses £35 + VAT

CITB Test

Sit the CITB Health, Safety and Environment Touchscreen Test at our Bargeddie office, on weekdays or weekends.
