Courses · CITB Test

The Touch­screen Test.

The CITB Health, Safety and Environment Test, sat at our Bargeddie office in Glasgow on weekdays and weekends.

Call 0141 771 0040 Send an enquiry

What to expect

50 questions.
45 minutes.

Construction plant operators applying for a CPCS or CSCS card must have passed this test within the previous two years.

Format

On computer

A 45-minute computer-based test with 50 multiple-choice questions: 12 behavioural case-study questions and 38 knowledge questions.

Test types

Operatives, Specialists, Managers

The Operatives test is the most common and is the one for site workers.

Revision

Study materials

A question and answer book, DVD, downloads and a mobile app are available from high-street and online booksellers or from CITB.

On the day

Bring photo ID

Bring in-date photographic ID. Without it you cannot sit the test.
